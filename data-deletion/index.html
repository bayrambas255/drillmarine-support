Data deletion

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Drillmarine keeps no data about you on any server, and it has no account. There is nothing for us to delete. All game data is on your device, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

Delete one campaign

Open Campaigns on the title screen, choose the slot and tap Delete campaign. That slot's world and progress are deleted for good. Your pearls and supplies are kept.

Delete everything

Delete the game: touch and hold its icon on the Home Screen, choose Remove App, then Delete App. This removes every campaign, your pearls and supplies, and your settings for good.

Backups

If the game is part of a device backup (iCloud or a computer), that copy disappears when the backup is deleted or replaced by a newer one. You manage your backups in the iOS Settings app.

Emails

If you have written to us and want your messages deleted, tell us at bayrambas255@icloud.com and we will delete them.

Purchase records

Your purchase history is kept by Apple, not by us. You can see it in your Apple Account.
