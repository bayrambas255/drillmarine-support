Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short

Drillmarine does not collect personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Drillmarine is an iOS game published by Bayram Baş, an individual developer in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the little personal data described in this policy. Contact: bayrambas255@icloud.com.

2. Data we collect through the game

None. The game has no servers of ours to talk to and sends nothing to us. It does not ask for your name, email address, age, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not read the advertising identifier.

3. What is saved on your device

The game saves the following in its own storage on your device, and nowhere else:

  • your campaigns (up to three slots): the world, your progress, upgrades and quests;
  • your wallet: pearls, supplies, captain level, daily rewards and contracts;
  • a list of the purchases already delivered, so that each one is paid out exactly once;
  • your settings: language, sound, controls, haptics, hints and the reminder switch.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It can be part of your own device backup (iCloud or a computer), which is covered by your agreement with Apple.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The game receives a confirmation from the App Store that a purchase was completed, with a transaction number, and keeps it on your device to deliver the items.

5. Reminders

The game can schedule one reminder a day ("Your daily reward is ready"). It is created on the device itself: no push service is used and nothing is sent to a server. iOS delivers it quietly to Notification Center and lets you keep it or turn it off. You can switch it off in the game's Settings (Daily reward reminder) or in the iOS Settings app under Notifications.

6. Apple and other parties

The game contains no third-party software for advertising, analytics or social networks, and we do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Apple processes data about your device, your downloads and your purchases under its own policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). Apple shows us sales and download totals, and, if you have agreed on your device to share analytics with app developers, crash and usage statistics. These reports do not identify you.

7. When you write to us

If you send us an email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report (our legitimate interest in supporting players). The messages are kept in our mailbox, which is provided by Apple (iCloud Mail), for as long as the conversation needs and are deleted when you ask us to.

8. This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when you open a page and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Children

The game does not collect data from anyone, children included. Purchases can be limited by a parent or guardian with Ask to Buy and Screen Time on the device.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Turkish KVKK, or California law), you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. As the game sends us nothing, this can only concern emails you have sent us. Write to bayrambas255@icloud.com and we will answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority of your country.

11. Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes all of its data from your device. Details are on the data deletion page.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts to collect data, this page and the App Store privacy details are updated before that version is released.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: bayrambas255@icloud.com.
